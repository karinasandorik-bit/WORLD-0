# WORLD-1 / TRIAL-1 PREREGISTRATION

## Question
Can identical adaptive agents learn when costly non-local information is worth acquiring, without being given an information-value rule or assigned roles?

## Hidden physics
At each tick the world samples a regime R in {LOCAL, DISTRIBUTED, DECOY}.
Each agent receives a local binary clue plus source offers. The correct binary action is generated by the hidden state.
- LOCAL: the local clue is sufficient.
- DISTRIBUTED: one offered non-local measurement contains additional decision-relevant information.
- DECOY: offered non-local measurements are conditionally non-informative/noisy.

Agents are not given R, source reliability, or a VOI formula.

## Agents
3 identical tabular Q-learning agents.
Same state encoding, action space, hyperparameters, initial Q values, initial budget and update rule.
No permanent roles, agent-specific priors, LLMs, cross-seed memory, or hand-coded rule saying when to acquire.

## Two-stage action
Stage 1: ACQUIRE(source_j) or SKIP.
Stage 2: ACT(0) or ACT(1).
Acquisition has positive cost. Reward is prediction utility minus acquisition cost.
Budget cannot go below zero; unaffordable acquisition is rejected by WorldKernel.

## Frozen confirmatory design
Training seeds: 0..199.
OOS evaluation seeds: 1000..1199.
Training and OOS worlds are disjoint.
Ticks per seed: 200.
Primary arm: MARKET.
Causal controls:
- FREE: identical measurements, acquisition cost = 0.
- NO_ACQUIRE: acquisition unavailable.
- SHUFFLED_VALUE: same acquisition interface and cost as MARKET, but acquired values are permuted across ticks within source.

## Primary estimand
OOS net utility:
Delta_market = U(MARKET) - U(NO_ACQUIRE).

Support requires mean paired Delta_market > 0 with a 95% paired bootstrap CI excluding 0.

## Mechanism tests
1. MARKET must outperform SHUFFLED_VALUE OOS under the same paired criterion.
2. Selectivity: acquisition rate must be higher in DISTRIBUTED than in LOCAL and DECOY regimes OOS.
3. Realized counterfactual VOI: among acquired measurements, mean [utility_with_acquired_value - utility_under_same_policy_without_value - cost] must be > 0 OOS.
4. Acquisition must not collapse to always-buy: OOS acquisition rate in MARKET must be strictly between 0.05 and 0.95.

## Secondary
Accuracy, acquisition cost, budget survival, per-regime utility, source-choice entropy, Q-policy stability.

## Integrity
Canonical Trial-1 physics, policy, hyperparameters, seeds, reward, costs, arms and metrics are SHA-256 hashed by executable code. Every event records spec_hash.
Changes after freeze create Trial-1b or later; they cannot repair Trial-1.

## Interpretation
PASS supports only: agents learned a selective costly-information acquisition policy that improved OOS net utility in WORLD-1.
It does NOT establish invention of new measurements, spontaneous semantic communication, specialization, collective intelligence, AGI, or consciousness.

## End states
PASS with preregistered causal controls -> MODEL CHANGED.
Failure of primary or mechanism criteria -> STOP for Trial-1 and retain the failure.
External-world consequences are absent, so WORLD CHANGED is not an allowed interpretation for this synthetic trial.
